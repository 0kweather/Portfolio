alex bronzini-vender is a freelance writer from New York, NY. He is currently an editorial intern at the Washington Monthly and a member of the editorial board of The Harvard Crimson. His writing has appeared in The New York Times, The Guardian, New York, POLITICO Magazine, The Baffler, Jacobin, and elsewhere. At Harvard College, he studies history and government.
